# Gridiron Rivals

A self-contained local two-player American football browser game. No server, build step, or external assets are required.

Open `index.html` directly in a browser.

- Player 1: `WASD` to move, hold/release `X` to throw or kick off.
- Player 2: arrow keys to move, hold/release `Space` to throw or kick off.
- While controlling the quarterback, move up to aim at `WR1` or down to aim at `WR2`; the gold target line follows your read.
- Kickoffs launch before the opening drive and after touchdowns. The receiving player controls the returner once the ball is caught.
- Choose teams before the game, then both players call offense/defense plays before each snap.

Play the deployed game at [football.derrickhsu.com](https://football.derrickhsu.com).

Submit bugs through the [GitHub issue page](https://github.com/DERHSU/Football-Game/issues).

## Playbook

Offense: Quick Slants, Four Verts, RB Screen, and Power Run.

Defense: Lock Man, Standard Blitz, Zone Blitz, and Cover 2.
